# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Visual collectors: designers and makers who save references, pictures and quotes as they browse, and who recognise things by how they looked rather than by what they were called.

## Product Purpose

A browser extension for Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers that keeps links, pictures, quotes and notes together in one visual library, stored in the browser with no account, no server and no tracking. The library tab and the side panel are the primary surfaces; the landing page in `site/` is a secondary, marketing surface.

Success is that people find things again: the reference, the picture or the quote they saved turns up when they need it, and they actually reopen it.

The product name is still being decided. Folia is the working name on the landing page; the manifest and README still say Bookmark Manager. A phone app is planned but not started; it will get its own context when it exists.

## Positioning

It shows you what you forgot. Saved things age visibly, so the ones you never reopened stand out and come back to you.

## Brand Personality

Curious, crafted, quiet. Made with care, it rewards a closer look and is never loud. Opening the library should feel like stepping into a room of your own: personal and lived-in, like a studio shelf or a pinboard, not a database.

The closest reference is Cosmos and Mymind, for visual-first saving where the picture is the bookmark.

## Anti-references

Productivity SaaS: dashboards, counts, streaks, nudges and upsell banners.

## Design Principles

- **Bring back what was forgotten.** Age, fading and resurfacing are the point of the product, not decoration. Every screen should make it easy to see what has gone untouched and easy to bring it back.
- **A room, not a database.** Prefer the feel of a shelf, a pinboard or a contact sheet over tables, folders and counts.
- **Quiet craft.** Detail that rewards a closer look, with nothing that shouts, nags or competes for attention.
- **Judge it by finding things again.** A feature earns its place by helping someone recognise and reopen what they kept.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Text meets 4.5:1 contrast everywhere, including on fully aged items. Targets are at least 44px, everything works from the keyboard, and reduced motion turns off all animation and transitions.
