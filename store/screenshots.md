# Screenshot and tile plan

Store specs: screenshots 1280×800 (or 640×400), PNG or JPEG, no alpha, 1 to 5 of them. Small promo tile 440×280 is required. Use real-looking but non-private sample data (no personal bookmarks, no logged-in pages).

Suggested set, in order (the first is the one people see in search results):

1. **The library.** A full grid of covers in the default theme. Caption: "Bookmarks you want to look at".
2. **Saving a page.** The side panel open beside a page after pressing Alt+Shift+S, tags being added. Caption: "One click to save, tag as you go".
3. **Themes and dark mode.** The same library in another theme, or a split of light and dark. Caption: "Three themes, light and dark".
4. **Search by colour.** The colour search in use. Caption: "Find it by colour".
5. **Links from posts.** The review list of links found in a pasted post. Caption: "Pull every link out of a post".

Small promo tile (440×280): the product name, one cover collage, no small text.

How to capture: set the browser window to 1280×800 (or use DevTools device toolbar), load the built extension, fill the library with sample sites, and screenshot. I can help generate a sample-data seed and drive the capture if you want.
