Not only links. Whatever made you stop.

  • PagesToolbar icon or Alt+Shift+S. Saved with a screenshot of how it looked, logged-in pages included.
  • PicturesRight-click an image. A copy is kept, tied to the page it came from.
  • QuotesSelect text and right-click. The one paragraph worth keeping, linked back to where you read it.
  • NotesA thought of your own, with tags. The first line becomes its title.

Pick a room. Start with one page.

For Chrome and Edge. Your library stays in your browser: no account, no server, no tracking.
