Prefer the site’s own image, or a screenshot? One switch in the library changes what links show. Search also reads titles, tags, your notes and the words inside quotes, and an A to Z index lists every tag.

No account.No server.No tracking.

Your library is kept in your browser’s own storage, on your computer. Folia only goes online to do what you ask: fetching a link’s title and preview, checking links still work, and reading a public post you paste in.

Before you move in.

What Folia doesn’t do yet, too.

Does it cost anything?

No. Folia is free and open source under the MIT licence, and nothing is held back for a paid plan.

Will it sync between my computers?

Not yet, and there’s no phone app. To move your library, back it up on one computer and restore it on the other.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge and other browsers built on Chromium. Not Firefox or Safari for now.

Is it in the Chrome Web Store?

Not yet. For now you build it and load it yourself, which takes a few minutes. The steps are just below.

Does it keep a copy of the page?

It keeps a screenshot of how the page looked and the site’s preview image, not the full text of the page.

What if I uninstall it?

Uninstalling deletes the library along with the extension, because it only lives in your browser. Back it up first and you can restore it later.

Can I switch rooms later?

Any time, from the library’s menu. Each room comes in light and dark, and everything you’ve kept stays as it is.

Close a tab. Keep it anyway.

Folia isn’t in the web stores yet, so for now you load it yourself. It takes a few minutes and works in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers.

InstructionsFour steps
  1. Build itRun npm install, then npm run build. This makes a dist folder.
  2. Open your extensionsGo to chrome://extensions or edge://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Load itChoose Load unpacked and pick the dist folder.
  4. Pin it and keep somethingPin Folia to the toolbar, then press Alt+Shift+S on any page worth coming back to.
