Nothing is deleted. It just gets harder to ignore.

Folia keeps the pages, pictures, quotes and notes you come across, each saved the way it looked. Anything you haven’t opened in three months starts to show it, in whichever of the four rooms you keep your library.

  • Library yellows.Paper goes amber, dust settles, cards tilt off square. Dust off puts them right.
  • Contact sheet bleaches.Frames drain toward white, like a print left in the sun. Reprint develops one back in.
  • Covers sun-fade.The top of the cover goes pale and spotted, and it leans on its neighbour. Take down puts it back in print.
  • Colour field goes grey.First the colour drains, then the glow goes out. Bring back lets it bloom.

Start with one page. See it in a year.

Free and open source, for Chrome and Edge. No account, no server, no tracking: your library stays in your browser.
