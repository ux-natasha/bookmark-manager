← → ↻ folia — keep the tabs, close the tabs

42 tabs open, because closing one feels like losing it.

Folia keeps them for you, saved the way they looked, in a library that lives in your browser. Then you can close them. All of them.

See what else it keeps

The ones you never open again start to look it.

Saving is the easy part. The hard part is remembering you saved it. So anything you haven’t opened in three months slowly ages, in plain sight, until you either come back to it or let it go.

Open it and it comes back to how it was. Or press Refresh my memory and Folia brings three forgotten things to the front.

No account.No server.No tracking.

Your library lives in your browser’s own storage, on your computer. Folia only goes online to fetch a page’s title and preview, and to check your links still work. Free and open source, for Chrome and Edge.
